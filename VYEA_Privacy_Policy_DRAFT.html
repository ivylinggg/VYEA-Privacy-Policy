Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Application: VYEA

This Privacy Policy explains how VYEA ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the VYEA mobile application and related services. VYEA provides personal colour analysis, wardrobe organisation and AI-assisted personal styling features.

1. Information you may provide

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, VYEA may process:

  • Account and profile information: name or display name, email address, account identifier and profile details.
  • Photos and images: photographs you choose to upload, including face, personal, clothing or wardrobe images, where a feature requests them.
  • Colour and styling information: colour-analysis results, colour season, style preferences, clothing preferences and selections.
  • Wardrobe information: clothing images, categories, descriptions, favourites and saved outfit information.
  • Technical information: app version, device and operating-system information, diagnostic information and security logs, where collected by the app or its service providers.

The precise information processed depends on the app features enabled in the version you use.

2. How we use information

We use information, as applicable, to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, provide colour analysis and styling features, organise wardrobe content, process images submitted by users, save preferences and results, maintain security, troubleshoot problems and operate the application.

3. AI features and third-party AI services

VYEA uses AI-assisted features. The AI services identified by the developer are Anthropic Claude and OpenAI services associated with ChatGPT. When you choose to use a feature that relies on an AI service, relevant prompts, images or other information needed for that feature may be sent to the applicable provider and processed to return the requested result.
